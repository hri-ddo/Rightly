## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an atmosphere of dignified authority, institutional permanence, and warm psychological safety. Tailored for citizens navigating sensitive legal and psychological distress in Bangladesh, the interface rejects cold, clinical sterility in favor of mid-century legal gravitas harmonized with contemporary SaaS precision.

### Aesthetic Foundation
- **Editorial Vintage Modernism:** Draws inspiration from mahogany-lined law archives, heavyweight parchment documents, and classical constitutional texts, executed through razor-sharp, modern typographic contrast.
- **Sensory Reassurance:** Balances deep carbon ink and aged cream surfaces with a distinguished mustard accent. The aesthetic communicates an unshakeable institutional foundation alongside human warmth.
- **Dignified Discretion:** Built specifically to reduce cognitive load and panic during high-stakes inquiries, featuring dedicated, instant-safety escape routes and official accreditation seals that project unquestioned legal validity.

## Colors

The palette employs deep ink pigments and tactile paper foundations to evoke the physical permanence of traditional legal volumes while maintaining pristine digital contrast.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Accent (`#E5B83F`):** Architectural mustard yellow. Used selectively for key conversion points, critical highlights, and active states. It should feel like stamped gilt lettering on leather-bound books.
- **Deep Ebony / Ink (`#20201E`):** The foundational text and structural anchor tone. Replaces standard digital black with an organic, ink-rich charcoal that softens eye strain while preserving legal weight.
- **Parchment Base (`#F7F3E9`):** A warm, tactile cream base that serves as the canvas, eliminating stark screen glare and fostering psychological comfort.
- **Muted Parchment Substrate (`#E8DFCC`):** Border definition, subtle panel fills, divider lines, and card containers.
- **Muted Olive (`#74735B`):** Tertiary supporting tone for contextual meta-information, secondary badges, and passive legal metadata.
- **Status Accents:**
  - **Success Green (`#47785D`):** Deep botanical green signifying verified legal status, completed filings, and secure encryption.
  - **Alert Red (`#B94B43`):** Subdued brick red for critical legal deadlines, emergency helplines, and safety exits.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy creates a dialogue between historic judicial gravitas and modern institutional clarity.

- **Headlines (Playfair Display):** Conveys the intellectual weight of classic constitutional doctrine, statutes, and high-bench chambers. Used for major editorial statements, primary article headings, and trust-establishing section entries.
- **Body & Functional Labels (Inter):** Highly legible, neutral sans-serif designed for intensive reading, complex legal documentation, multi-step intake processes, and clear credential badges.
- **Treatment Rules:** Section caps and badge tags must use `label-md` or `label-sm` with upper-case transformation and expanded letter spacing (`0.05em`–`0.08em`) to mirror letterpress editorial conventions.

## Layout & Spacing

A structured 8-point spatial rhythm governs all alignments, creating a steady, disciplined architecture reminiscent of an organized legal brief.

### Layout Rules
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column fluid grid, max container width `1280px`, with `margin: 3rem` and `gutter: 1.5rem`.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column fluid grid, with `margin: 2rem` and `gutter: 1.25rem`.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid grid, with `margin-mobile: 1.25rem` and `gutter-mobile: 1rem`.

### Spatial Philosophy
Negative space is deliberately generous around sensitive reading areas to lower heart rate and anxiety during legal queries. Dense configurations are reserved strictly for tabular legal dockets, accreditation credentials, and directory listings.

## Elevation & Depth

To avoid plastic or artificial digital effects, elevation relies primarily on tactile surface tone layers, crisp physical document borders, and warm, diffused ambient shadows.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Natural parchment background (`#F7F3E9`). Completely flat.
- **Level 1 (Card & Section Containers):** Crisp 1px solid border (`#E8DFCC`), crisp background (`#FFFFFF`), with an ultra-subtle warm shadow: `0px 2px 8px rgba(32, 32, 30, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating & Modals):** 1px solid border (`#E8DFCC`), surface (`#FFFFFF`), with a layered archival drop shadow: `0px 8px 24px -4px rgba(32, 32, 30, 0.08), 0px 2px 6px -1px rgba(32, 32, 30, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Safety Hotline / Sticky Header):** Fixed positioning with high-contrast anchor styling. The emergency bar uses solid `#20201E` or crisp bordered glass (`rgba(247, 243, 233, 0.92)` with `8px` backdrop blur) to maintain permanent clarity without obscuring primary content.

## Shapes

The design system employs a soft, tailored corner treatment (`roundedness: 1`). 

- **Containers, Cards, & Modals:** Standard corner radius of `4px` (`0.25rem`). This provides structural rigor reminiscent of archival legal paper and bound books while eliminating harsh abrasive corners.
- **Buttons, Text Inputs, & Tabs:** Crisp `4px` border radius to maintain utilitarian confidence and alignment consistency.
- **Verification Badges & Pills:** Specialized full-rounded pill shapes (`9999px`) used exclusively for accreditation seals, status indicators, and legal credential tags to cleanly distinguish metadata from clickable action blocks.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#E5B83F`, label `#20201E` (`weight: 600`), 1px solid `#E5B83F`, 4px radius. Hover: darkened mustard tone (`#D4A52A`).
- **Secondary / Formal:** Background `#20201E`, label `#F7F3E9`, 1px solid `#20201E`. Hover: `#353431`.
- **Outlined / Judicial:** Background transparent, label `#20201E`, 1px solid `#E8DFCC`. Hover: `#FFFFFF`, border `#20201E`.
- **Safety / Quick Exit:** Immediate high-contrast escape button. Distinctive, fixed placement (top right or persistent utility bar). Solid `#B94B43` or high-contrast outlined warning state with rapid redirect capabilities.

### Accreditation & Legal Badges
- High-integrity pill badges designed to establish trust immediately:
  - *Bangladesh Bar Council Verified:* Soft parchment fill `#F2EDE1`, border 1px solid `#74735B`, icon in `#47785D`, typography `label-sm` uppercase.
  - *BMDC Certified Psychiatrist:* Tone-matched green boundary (`#47785D/15`), fill `#FFFFFF`, text `#47785D`.
  - *NID Verified:* Muted olive boundary, text `#20201E`.

### Inputs & Form Fields
- Surface: `#FFFFFF`.
- Border: 1px solid `#E8DFCC`.
- Focus State: Border 1px solid `#20201E`, subtle warm glow ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(229, 184, 63, 0.25)`.
- Helper & Validation Text: Rendered in `body-sm` using `#74735B` for instructional notes, and `#B94B43` for error alerts.

### Cards & Legal Brief Panels
- Surface `#FFFFFF`, surrounded by a 1px solid border (`#E8DFCC`), inner spacing of `space-lg` (`1.5rem`).
- Header cards feature a quiet 1px horizontal rule separating case titles from supporting case law or clinical notes.

### Safety Hotline Banner
- A persistent strip fixed to the top or bottom of the viewport:
  - Background `#20201E`, foreground text `#F7F3E9`.
  - Emphasized direct call links: National Emergency Service (999), Child & Women Helpline (109), Mental Health Support (Kaan Pete Roi).
  - Paired with an immediate, high-contrast, one-click `Quick Exit` button that instantly replaces the browser history and redirects to an innocuous utility page.
